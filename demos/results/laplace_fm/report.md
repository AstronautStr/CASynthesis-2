# Laplace FM -- hand-over measurements

REQ `memory/req-laplace-fm-2026-09-20.md` section 6. Generated by `demos/laplace_fm_report.py` on the pinned sound code. Block 352 samples = 7.98 ms; release 3 blocks = 23.95 ms; oversampling x8, 625 FIR taps, delay 39 output samples (0.88 ms); DC blocker at 5 Hz.

## 1. The input modes

- all 49 generations of the 12 s Jam: the engine's (f_j, a_j) equal the baseline `analyse` (worst |df| 0 Hz, worst |da| 0)
- the 6 objects of the three phases equal the Researcher preflight (worst ratio 0, worst weight 0; its core hash matches: True)
- the bench gain never reaches the index: the FM sum before the gain is identical at two master gains (True)

## 2. The law

- one modulator (f 190.5256 Hz, beta 1.7): every line of the engine's expansion equals J_n(beta) to 0, and the phase formula equals the signal rebuilt from those lines to 1.6e-13
- the Researcher's published lines of the Jam at I = 1 (6 objects): worst difference 2.8e-12
- four non-integer frequencies with unequal weights [1.0, 0.62, 0.31, 0.145] at depth 2.5: -157.1 dB against the phase formula at 32x
- two modulators on 311 Hz with the SAME phase: indices add to 1.3 (-158.5 dB against one modulator of that index); with a 1.1 rad offset they add as vectors to 1.1193 (-159.5 dB), and the two cases differ by 3.0 dB -- coherent, never a sum of powers
- stationary peak deviation at f0 4000 Hz, one modulator 50 Hz, beta 10: measured 4500.0 / 3500.0 Hz around a mean of 4000.0 Hz, i.e. +-500 Hz as beta*f_m asks
- ONE carrier modulated jointly: -158.1 dB against the joint formula and only -2.0 dB against a sum of separately modulated carriers of the same total scale

## 3. Zero depth and DC

- FM depth 0 is the carrier sine at the figure's own scale A_object = 1.7321, through the same output filter: -173.7 dB against the reference; it is NOT the old Laplacian sum of sines (0.1 dB against side A)
- empty: silent at depths 0 / 1 / 4 (peak int16 0)
- one cell: silent at depths 0 / 1 / 4 (peak int16 0)
- a modulator at exactly f0 with a phase offset really does put a constant of 0.5767 into the sum; the fixed blocker leaves -4.3e-07 (-123 dB)
- a held constant through the blocker: 0.173 after 48 ms, 0.0384 after 96 ms, 0.000421 after 240 ms, 2.28e-07 after 479 ms; the closed form equals the scalar recursion to 6.1e-16

## Summary

- The modes are the baseline's and the preflight's; the index is beta = I * a with no normalisation and no gain in it.
- The phase law checks out against an independent Bessel expansion (2e-13), coincident modulators add coherently, and the carrier is modulated JOINTLY (-158 dB against the joint formula, -2 dB against a sum of separate carriers).
- Band: the reference converges (worst 16x vs 32x -165 dB, REQ -80 dB) and the shipped x8 is within -155 dB of it on still figures and -89 dB over the whole Jam with its transitions (REQ -60 dB).
- Levels: the three tracks sit within 0.03 dB, nothing clips, and R / Wave bank+Saw are byte-identical to the pinned record already listened to.
- Budget: p99 5.05 ms of the 7.98 ms block on both delivered scenes; Continue is exact including a generation change and a pause.
- Limits (section 6): the delivered scenes, a high note and n = 20 all stay inside the block; a dense random field leaves the budget (as the baseline does on the same field) -- the table says where, and no index or frequency is reduced to hide it.
- Nothing here says the FM sounds useful: that is the listening question of the catalog.
